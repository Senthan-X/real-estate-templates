# Aurelia Estates — Property Taxonomy

## Version 1 scope

- `LUX-VIL`: detached luxury villas and private estates
- `LUX-PEN`: penthouses and sky residences
- `LUX-APT`: premium apartments and serviced residences
- `LUX-TWN`: contemporary townhouses
- `LUX-DEV`: gated residential developments

## Asset roles

- `HERO`: immersive exterior with clear text-safe space
- `FEATURED`: primary editorial property cards
- `CATALOG`: secondary searchable property inventory
- `LIFESTYLE`: neighbourhood, interior, or ownership experience
- `PROCESS`: agents, viewings, contracts, or handover
- `RESERVE`: land, surveying, construction, and infrastructure assets retained for later series versions
